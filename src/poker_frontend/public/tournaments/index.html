Poker tournaments

Bullets Poker runs single-table sit-and-go's and scheduled multi-table tournaments, with either free play chips or ICP. Seating is shuffled with on-chain randomness.

Sit-and-go's

These start as soon as they fill, from heads-up duels to 9-player tables, and a new one opens automatically.

Scheduled tournaments

  • Hourly Freeroll: free to enter, with play-chip prizes.
  • Chip Rebuy Madness: every two hours, with play chips.
  • ICP Nightly: once a day, with an ICP buy-in.

Blinds rise on a schedule, and tables balance and merge down to a final table. Check the Tournaments page in the app for current events, entry costs and start times.

Late entry, rebuys and add-ons

Scheduled events accept late entries during their first levels. In rebuy events, a player who busts during the rebuy period gets 30 seconds to buy back in, and an add-on is sold around the end of that period.

Prizes

Tournament chips can't be cashed out. Prizes go straight to your wallet, and payouts scale with the number of entries.

Real-money play may be restricted where you live. Play responsibly. 18+.
