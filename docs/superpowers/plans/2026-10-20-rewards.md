# Rewards (Fish Buffet + Leaderboards) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Add ICP-only loyalty tiers with a cashback wheel, and weekly/monthly points and winnings leaderboards. Both are paid only from pools that get a fixed share of the house's rake.

**Architecture:**
- Two new backend modules hang off the existing hand and tournament hooks in `state.rs` and `tournament.rs`:
  - `rewards.rs`: funding split, points, tiers, decay, wheel.
  - `leaderboards.rs`: periods, boards, payouts.
- Persisted state:
  - Per player: in an `Option<RewardsAccount>` on `User` (stable memory).
  - Pools, board top lists and period markers: in one `Option<RewardsState>` heap field.
- The frontend gets a Rewards page with two tabs: Fish Buffet and Leaderboards.

**Tech Stack:** Rust (ic-cdk, candid), React + TypeScript, vitest.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-20-rewards-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- **ICP only.** Chip tables, chip tournaments and tournament *chips* earn nothing.
- **Funding split** of house rake (cash rake after the host's cut, plus ICP tournament fees at start):
  - Fish Buffet pool: 30%.
  - Leaderboard pool: 10%, split into four equal quarters (one per board).
  - Treasury: 60%. Rounding goes to the treasury.
- **Points:** 1 per 1,000 e8s of *attributed* rake (the full rake, including the host's part), with leftover e8s carried forward.
- **Tiers** (name, from, spin step, rate in bps):
  - Plankton 0 / 100 / 800
  - Shrimp 500 / 400 / 1100
  - Crab 2,500 / 1,500 / 1400
  - Octopus 10,000 / 6,000 / 1700
  - Dolphin 40,000 / 20,000 / 2000
  - Orca 150,000 / 70,000 / 2400
  - Shark 500,000 / 200,000 / 2800
- **Spin stake** is the *house share* of the attributed rake (×0.8 in hosted rooms).
- **Banked spins:** at most 50. The spin history keeps the last 20.
- **Wheel:**
  - Multipliers 0.5×, 1×, 1.5×, 2×, 5×, 10×.
  - The weights give an expected value of exactly 1.0, and 10× is the rarest.
  - Paid amount = min(stake × rate × mult, pool).
- **Decay:** after 7 full days without earning, tier points × 0.95 per further day, applied lazily.
- **Periods:**
  - A week starts Monday 00:00 UTC. A month starts on the 1st at 00:00 UTC.
  - Labels look like `2026-W42` (ISO week) and `2026-10`.
- **Boards:** Weekly Points, Weekly Winnings, Monthly Points, Monthly Winnings.
- **Prizes:** top 10 get 30/20/13/9/7/6/5/4/3/3%. Ties go to whoever reached the value first.
- **Winnings boards:**
  - Qualify with ≥200 hands a week or ≥500 a month, and net > 0.
  - Prize ≤ the rake that player paid in the period.
- **Unpaid prize money** stays in that board's quarter.
- **Medals:** the top 3 per board per period, plus a lobby chat announcement.
- **Persistence:** new `User` and heap fields are `Option<T>`. Regenerate candid with `UPDATE_CANDID=1 cargo test`.
- **Calls:** state changes are update calls via `caller()`. Reads are queries.
- **Commands:**
  - Run every cargo or npm command in WSL: `MSYS_NO_PATHCONV=1 wsl -d Ubuntu-24.04 -- bash -lc 'cd /mnt/c/BulletsPoker && …'`.
  - Backend tests: `cargo test`.
  - Frontend: `cd src/poker_frontend && npx vitest run && npm run build`.

## Review Focus

1. **Money conservation with pools.**
   - Wallets + tables + treasury + tournaments + jackpot + all pools must equal the ICP put in, including hosted rooms.
   - `house_stats().liabilities_total` and the solvency check must include the pools.
2. **Period close idempotency.**
   - A tick after an upgrade, several missed periods, or two ticks in the same second must never pay a board twice or pay the wrong period's list.
3. **A big pot crossing several spin steps at once,** or a tier change mid-hand.
   - Each banked spin uses the tier at the moment it was banked, and its stake is the right slice.
   - The bank caps at 50.
4. **A pool short at spin time.**
   - The spin pays what's in the pool, the result says it was capped, and the pool never goes negative.
5. **Tournament edges.**
   - A cancelled event (fees refunded) gives no points.
   - Rebuys and add-ons count toward the cost in the net result.
   - A finish is recorded once.
6. **Old snapshots and users without the new fields** decode and behave as zero.

  - `impl State { pub(crate) fn earn_rake(&mut self, p: &Principal, rake_e8s: u64, house_e8s: u64, now: u64) }`:
    1. Materialise decay.
    2. Add points (the carry and lifetime counters), the period points and the rake.
    3. Fill the meter.
    4. Bank a spin for each step crossed at the *current* tier. The stake is split evenly over the spins banked in this call, and the bank caps at 50: extra steps are dropped, with their stake.
    5. Call the leaderboard hook `self.on_points(p, now)`. Until Task 4 this is a no-op method in `leaderboards.rs`.
  - `pub(crate) fn record_icp_hand(&mut self, p: &Principal, net: i64, now: u64)` adds to the hands and net in the period counters. Task 4 hooks the board update in.
  - `pub fn attribute(rake: u64, players: &[(Principal, u64 /*committed*/)]) -> Vec<(Principal, u64)>`: proportional with floor, remainder to no one.
- In `after_table_change`, only for `currency == Icp && !in_tournament`:
  - Attribute the hand's `result.rake` by `committed`.
  - Each share's house part = `share - share * HOST_RAKE_SHARE_BPS / 10_000` when the table is a room, otherwise `share`.
  - Call `earn_rake` and `record_icp_hand(net)`.
- ICP tournament hands call `record_icp_hand(p, 0, now)`, which counts the hand for qualifying but not the net.
- At tournament start, each ICP entrant calls `earn_rake(p, fee, fee, now)`.
